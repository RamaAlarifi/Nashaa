# Nashaa user guide

Sign in with an authorised account. The navigation shows actions appropriate to the account role. Verification is platform approval, not legal identity certification. Business assessments, customer profiles and lead workspaces remain private except for the owner's existing explicit sharing choices.

## Business owners

1. Open My ideas, save a business idea and review its visibility. AI assessment generation shows feedback and preserves the last successful assessment if another attempt fails.
2. In the idea workspace, open Customer discovery. Choose business customers or consumer segments, generate a target profile and review its assumptions. Edit the audience, target industry, location, need and acquisition channels, then save. Revisit the profile after changing the business plan.
3. For business customers, import a permitted company CSV. Required headers are `company_name,industry,location,website,business_need`. Industry uses category identifiers from the application's industry catalogue; location may be blank. Include an accurate source/permission basis and confirm that the records may be used. Mark fictional data as fictional. Files are limited to 100 KB and 200 records; personal-contact columns are rejected. Existing company/location pairs are skipped rather than duplicated.
4. Review company suggestions and their reasons/source labels. Save useful customers. Consumer ideas instead show segment and acquisition-channel hypotheses and do not accept company-contact imports.
5. In the idea's Suggested investors section, save relevant investors. Saved leads collects both opportunity types. Open a lead, add private notes and save a follow-up status. Status changes do not automatically request contact or send a message.
6. Generate an outreach draft for a saved lead, review the business-specific claims, edit and save it, then copy the saved draft. Nashaa never sends it automatically. Generation failures preserve the lead and previous draft.
7. Publish a challenge, compare proposals and select an innovator. Open Collaborations to share progress. When the innovator marks work ready, review the work and confirm completion. Submit one rating for the completed collaboration.

## Innovators

Request verification, browse/filter challenges and submit a proposal with approach, deliverables, timeline and optional cost. After selection, open Collaborations. Both participants can add progress updates. Only the selected innovator marks work ready; the business owner confirms completion. A completed collaboration permits one rating from each participant. Existing contact permission and verification rules still govern messaging.

## Investors

Maintain investment preferences/history and inspect ranked registered project summaries. Scores show available fit factors, not financial advice or guaranteed suitability. Detailed assessments require separate, recipient- and version-specific owner approval. Contact preferences continue to control messaging.

## Reporting and administration

Use Report this record on a visible profile, idea, challenge, collaboration or message. Select a category and describe the issue; inspect My reports for its status and recorded decision. Reporting does not grant access to another user's private record.

Administrators open Administration to inspect the report queue, review the related record and record a decision with reasons. Permitted actions include participant account status changes, hiding/restoring ideas or challenges and removing a message. Account suspension/deactivation invalidates sessions. Review administrative action history and activity counts. Verification retains its own review screen and records audit actions.

Evaluation evidence accepts actual, consent-confirmed anonymous session observations. Report participant counts, roles, browsers and sample sizes. Do not enter demonstration outcomes as real evaluation results.

## Errors and saved information

Check highlighted fields when validation fails. CSV errors do not partially import the file. A duplicate lead opens the existing saved record. A failed AI request can be retried after the service becomes available; earlier saved work remains available. If an idea/profile/draft changes during generation, review the latest saved content and retry rather than overwriting it. Completed work is read-only for progress updates. Hidden content requires an administrator's restoration; ordinary owner edits do not undo moderation.
